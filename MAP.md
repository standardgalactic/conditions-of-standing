# Map to the corpus

This map relates the existing essays to the keystone document, `main.tex`. Each essay has one primary category and, where it applies, short cross-references to the cases or propositions it also touches, so that a single classification does not suggest that an essay has only one relationship to the keystone. The assignments are provisional. They rest on titles and recorded descriptions and not on a fresh reading of each text, and each should be confirmed against the essay before this map is treated as settled.

## Worked instances

These essays treat a subject that one of the five cases in Section 4 analyzes, or that one of the propositions bears on directly. They keep their own arguments and gain a pointer to the keystone for the shared vocabulary, and where an essay repeats a definition that the keystone now fixes, the repetition can be shortened.

*Contrast as Control* is the source for the self-distillation case (Section 4.1), through its analysis of a proxy-teacher mechanism; it also touches the fifth proposition. *Semantic Geometry and Corrective Navigation* is the source for the repair-ledger case (Section 4.2), and it touches the third and fifth propositions. *Authorization and Its Remainder* is the source for the economic-authorization case (Section 4.3), and it touches the first and second propositions. *Echo Chamber* is the source for the branching-commitment case (Section 4.5), and it touches the fourth proposition. *The Reversibility Gate* distinguishes observation, delay and action under unsettled claims; its primary relation is to the second and fourth propositions, and it is the natural companion to the reversibility discussion in Section 3. *The Domain and Its Establishment* concerns information acquisition and responsibility; it bears on the roles of evidence and of the scope in the standing relation, and cross-refers to Section 2. *The Unit of Scrutiny* concerns workflow authorship and what is taken as the unit that is assessed, and it bears on the scope argument and on the third proposition. *Evidentiary Decoupling* concerns the alignment of a signal with the outcome it is taken to indicate; it bears on the third and fifth propositions.

There is no essay on reconstructive memory in the corpus that this map can assign, and the fourth case (Section 4.4) was written without one.

## Technical references

These supply formal machinery that the keystone only states. They are kept external at first. Merging them would recreate the problem the repository exists to solve, a compact entry point growing into a comprehensive formal system. A definition or result migrates into the keystone only when one of the five propositions requires it, and each migration is recorded in the log at the end of this file.

*Verification Without Correspondence* treats semantic fidelity between a formal statement and what it was meant to express, which is the bridge between the computational and epistemic senses of standing described in Section 2. *The Witness Plane* formalizes a pipeline of admissibility, verification and commitment in the author's process calculus and is the closest existing formal relative of the five transitions; it should be read against Section 1 before either text is revised. *The Calculus of Disposition* concerns commitment, refusal and collapse in the same calculus and relates to the fourth proposition. *Geometry of Scope* develops context and visibility formally and relates to the scope argument of the second proposition. *Increments Are Not Contributions* distinguishes sequential ablation from intrinsic contribution and relates to the fifth proposition, since both concern what a summary of a record can and cannot determine. *Retraction Is an Operation* treats retraction as an operation in its own right and relates to the fourth proposition and to the open question of revocation raised in Section 5.

## Separate lines of work

These are cited and not absorbed. They overlap the keystone in subject and differ in aim, and folding them in would blur both.

*The Cost of Future Admissibility* gives the treatment of commitment and the preservation of future options on which the fourth proposition relies, and it is cited and not restated. *Degenerate Ledgers* and *Consistency Without Contribution* treat ledgers and productivity, and relate to the fifth proposition without being instances of it. *Adversaria*, the specification for a public claim graph, is an implementation target for a record in which standing is tracked and not only inscription; it is the nearest practical application of the vocabulary. *Synthetic Testimony* and *The Person Behind the Interface* concern the attribution of mind and the genre of consciousness claims, which the keystone declines to treat as transitions in Section 1 and which raise questions about authorship and provenance that the keystone's account of inscription does not settle. *Notes Left on a Shared Board* treats coordination among agents through a shared record and relates to inscription and to de facto commitment.

*The Price of Answerability* is reserved as a later consequence of the keystone. Its central argument concerns what happens to the cost of maintaining a shared record when the cost of generating candidates falls and the cost of evaluating them does not, and it presupposes the definition of standing given here.
